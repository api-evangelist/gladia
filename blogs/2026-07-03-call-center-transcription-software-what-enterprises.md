Contact centers should evaluate transcription software based on real-world multilingual accuracy rather than clean-audio benchmarks, prioritizing all-inclusive per-hour pricing and robust data sovereignty compliance. Key selection criteria include testing on actual BPO call samples, speaker diarization for QA reliability, and vendors bundling sentiment analysis and entity extraction into base rates.
