Word Error Rate (WER) is the accuracy ceiling for every downstream feature including sentiment scoring, CRM enrichment, and compliance triggers. A 5% WER on a 5-minute call produces roughly 38 incorrect words, concentrated on product names, customer names, and compliance phrases the conversation intelligence stack depends on.
