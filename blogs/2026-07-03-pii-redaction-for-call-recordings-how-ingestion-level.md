Automated, ingestion-level PII redaction removes sensitive payment data from call recordings before storage, helping contact centers maintain PCI DSS compliance while reducing audit scope compared to legacy pause-and-resume systems. The approach pairs high-accuracy speech-to-text with entity detection to mask both audio and transcripts.
